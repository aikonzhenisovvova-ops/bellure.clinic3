Frequently asked questions

Do I need a consultation before booking a treatment?

Yes — every first visit starts with a doctor’s examination, even for simple treatments like cleansing.

How do I know which treatment is right for me?

The doctor recommends a plan after examining your skin. You are never asked to decide from a menu alone.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment.

Does the form on this page book a real appointment?

No. This website is a static demonstration, so the form only checks your entries in the browser. To arrange a real visit, use the phone number or email address on this page.
